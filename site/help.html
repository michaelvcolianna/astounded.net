Get help

What I can help with

  • Figuring out which choice looks like it fits your needs
  • Choosing hosting
  • Setting up a site, including a content management system (CMS)
  • Writing code for a feature your site is missing
  • Setting up backups
  • Maintaining a static site
  • Troubleshooting and fixing some problems on a site
  • Moving your site from one platform to another

What I can’t help with

  • Logos or any kind of design
  • Writing content for your site
  • Any kind of advertising or marketing
  • Any questions related to selling stuff online
  • Emergency support at any hour
  • Hosting your site for you (with possible exceptions)

What it costs

Nothing! I want to help my fellow authors out. Times are tough for most of us right now for all kinds of reasons. Boosting signals and maybe being able to buy a book here or there doesn't fulfill the need I have to help out, and this website stuff does.

How to reach me

You can email me at mvc@astounded.net. If you're on Bluesky, you should be able to DM me (@mvc.ink).

Make sure to let me know:

  • Your website address & where it's running (if you have one & you know the answer)
  • What you're looking to do (if a new site) or any pain points you've got (if an existing site).
  • Any kind of deadline. It'll help me prioritize.

I may not be able to help. If that's the case, I'll at least try to point you in the right direction.
