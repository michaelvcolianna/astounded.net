Accessibility

My goal

This site aims to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AAA, the highest level. I haven't audited it fully yet.

Information

  • Plain HTML, no scripts.
  • No tracking, analytics, or cookies.
  • No images, audio, or video.
  • No animations.
  • Legible, resizable text.
  • Appropriate contrast for all. (21:1 for copy, 9.4:1 for links.)
  • Keyboard navigation and appropriate focus.
  • A glossary for explanation of technical terms.

Known problems

None at the moment.

Report a problem

If you encounter anything on the site that is difficult to use, let me know via mvc@astounded.net. If possible, letm eknow the page address and what went wrong.
