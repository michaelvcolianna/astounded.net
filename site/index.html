Websites for authors

What this site is

Hi, I'm MVC. I'm an author but I'm also a web developer. You should have your own website! Social media is fine, but there should be a place that's yours, that you have control over. Your website doesn't have to look great, it just has to have useful information and be accessible to everyone. I put this site together for two reasons.

  1. To provide other authors with helpful information about creating their own website.
  2. To let other authors know – I'll help you out for free!

TL;DR

If you already have some idea about what you want for a website, but are hestitant due to effort/cost, the best advice I can give you is to go for what seems the easiest and lowest cost. You can, in most cases, move elsewhere if you don't like your choice.

Most authors go with a WordPress.com page. It gets the job done.

But if you have questions, aren't sure which questions to ask, don't know if something is too expensive, or anything else: I'll help you out! You can also check out the glossary.
