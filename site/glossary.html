Glossary

Plain definitions of some of the terms used on this site. Hopefully. If anything's unclear, let me know!

CMS (content management system)
Where you write and edit all of the pages for your website, ideally without writing any code.
Theme
Your site's design. Often times, ready-made and you just fill in the information, but sometimes specially crafted.
Domain name
Your site's address on the web. Like this one, astounded.net. You pay money yearly to "rent" it.
DNS (domain name system)
Sort of like an address book for the web. It takes website.com and points you to the correct web host.
Export
A way to download your content (pages, images, etc.) so it can be saved and/or moved elsewhere.
Headless CMS
WordPress, for example, has the content and the theme together. A headless CMS is just the content, which provides freedom for how that content looks.
Hosting
A computer (or a section of that computer) that you "rent" and stores your website.
Link-in-bio page
A single web page that lists your links, often used from a social media profile.
Plugin
A small add-on that adds a feature to your website. Most frequently associated with WordPress.
Static site
A bunch of HTML files. You either edit them by hand or use a static site generator. But nothing is on-demand so not only is it fast but there's nothing to hack.
Static site generator (SSG)
A tool that takes your content and a design, then creates a bunch of HTML files to place on your host.
Transaction fee
A share of each sale that a platform keeps.
